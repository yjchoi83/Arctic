terminals, civil airstrips, SAR/response stations, hydrographic survey polygons. Several NSR ports
(Dikson, Tiksi, Pevek, Anadyr, Sabetta area) have military infrastructure nearby; those footprints
are excluded by polygon masking and no card reports asset-level military inference. Aggregate,
public-data, safety-and-transparency framing only.
